# Operating-case matrix

Generated by `python python/build_operating_matrix.py`. Editable case definitions
are in that script; the CSV retains numeric values for both architectures.
These are calculated volume/timing budgets, **not new hydraulic simulations**.

See [goal and concept comparison](GOAL_AND_CONCEPT_MATRIX.md) for requirements,
pressure cases, source provenance and selection criteria.

## Definitions and assumptions

- Q is mean flow through each complete series limb, in L/min. Left and right
  contributions are not added to report cardiac output.
- h is pulses/min at each arterial outlet. One delivery to each outlet occurs
  per simulated heart period. Every delivery cycle includes filling and ejection.
- 1A uses one chamber for two sequential duties: physical cycle rate = 2h.
  1B uses two chambers: physical cycle rate = h per chamber.
- Required net liquid volume per circuit pulse [mL] = 1000 Q / h.
- Reference geometric stroke = 66.6667 mL, derived from 5 L/min at 75/min.
  It remains illustrative, not a selected cylinder or guaranteed net delivery.
- Listed cycle times assume equal time allocation for R/L duties in 1A.
  They include the total available budget: filling, ejection and any switching
  or dwell must fit within it. Unequal R/L allocations remain possible.
- Ideal diversion = 1 − required volume/reference stroke, where non-negative.
  It is an accounting fraction, not an implemented valve command. Gas storage
  and real valve dynamics require the net volume to be calculated/measured.
- No listed flow/rate pair automatically passes waveform, pressure, filling,
  force or supply constraints. PDS combinations require individual approval.

## Time and throughput at the nominal and high-rate references

| Case / architecture | Time per physical delivery cycle (s) | Target liquid processed by each chamber (L/min) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 / 1A | 0.4000 | 10.00 |
| C01 / 1B | 0.8000 | 5.00 |
| C07 / 1A | 0.1667 | 10.00 |
| C07 / 1B | 0.3333 | 5.00 |

At 5 L/min circuit flow, 1A processes 10 L/min of liquid passes because
the circulating liquid goes through it once for each ventricular duty.
The circuit output remains 5 L/min. Each 1B chamber processes 5 L/min.
These are target net passes, excluding any extra bypass throughput.

For the illustrative 40% advance fraction, multiply the available cycle
time by 0.4 to obtain advance time; actual outlet ejection duration can
differ because of gas compression and valve dynamics. No dwell is reserved
by the current motion function.

## Model coverage and completion criteria

The archived sweep covers one physical chamber at 40–180 cycles/min under
constant pressures. It does not represent 1A switching between loads, even
where 2h is within that rate range. It does not represent coupled 1B loops.
In particular, **1A at 180 bpm requires 360 physical cycles/min; no force
result has been calculated for that architecture or rate here.**

For each retained case, record target and actual mean flow, arterial pulse
rate, net volumes, chamber and outlet pressure histories, maximum hydraulic
force, minimum liquid inventory, gas state, switching loss, reservoir drift
and status. Include numerical verification and measurement uncertainty.
Only after these checks can an operating point be called hydraulically
feasible under the stated assumptions. Physical calibration and independent
validation remain later, separate steps.
